# Round 4: ideas from random sampling (to avoid "collapsing to the mean")

Every seed below traces back to a randomly sampled real item, not to an LLM's first thoughts:
- 212 `Special:Random` Wikipedia landings, about 100 `Special:RandomInCategory` hits, and a random walk through outbound links.
- About 140 random items from the Met Museum API, the Art Institute of Chicago, arXiv (random offsets in 15 odd categories), the Internet Archive, OEIS, xkcd and old US patents.

Most random hits were stubs (football seasons, moths, railway stations). The seeds come from the roughly 15% that had a mechanism a player could manipulate. Source tags: R = `Special:Random`, C = random-in-category, W = reached on the random walk, X = non-Wikipedia random source.

Scores are 1–5 for **H**ook, **D**epth, **V**isual potential and **N**ovelty against our set and the web (to be confirmed by a prior-art agent). ★ marks the build shortlist.

## Process for the shortlist
1. A prior-art and design agent searches the web for anything similar and proposes ways to make each one more fun.
2. Build.
3. Adversarial full-playthrough review by subagents.
4. Optimisation pass by subagents.

## Status
Built, playtested and optimised: Reduction Woodcut, Lock Flight, Belt Trick, Swarm Season (all on the hub under Round four).
Known open items: Woodcut levels 1–5 still accept most ink orders; Lock Flight levels 5, 10 and 16 don't reward a policy choice; Belt Trick's anti-twister level is shallow; Swarm's queen hunt is slow on phones.
