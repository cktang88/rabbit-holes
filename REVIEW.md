# Fresh-eyes review: sense and depth

Four independent critics, none of whom built the games, played each one cold. They judged it on two things:
- **Sense:** do you understand within a minute what to do and why things happen?
- **Depth:** are there decisions, trade-offs, mastery and an hour of play?

Scores are 1–5. Each verdict became a brief for the game's owner, and "rethink" means the core loop is being redesigned.

| Game | Sense | Depth | Verdict | Main problem | Fix in progress |
